# CaptureCat iOS — App Store creative

Everything here is generated by code in `source/`. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
App Store name "Screen Recorder: CaptureCat", subtitle "Edit and share screen videos".

## Regenerate

Needs a Debug build of the app WITH the store fixture (see "App changes") on
simulators named `Store …` (iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPad Pro 13" (M5), iPhone Duo on the
`iOS-27-2-iPhone19-4` runtime), light appearance, status bar overridden to 9:41.

```
S=apps/ios/AppStoreAssets/source
$S/scripts/capture-shots.sh <iphone-udid> iphone
$S/scripts/capture-shots.sh <ipad-udid> ipad
$S/scripts/capture-shots.sh <duo-udid> duo-outer 1      # Duo folded (outer = display 1)
$S/scripts/capture-shots.sh <duo-udid> duo-inner 3      # after unfolding in Device Hub (inner = display 3)
$S/scripts/record-tour.sh <iphone-udid> iphone-tour
$S/scripts/record-tour.sh <ipad-udid> ipad-tour         # then check footage/ipad-tour.cuts.json
cd apps/ios/AppStoreAssets
python3 source/scripts/render.py                       # or --only iphone-6.9,header --locales ja
python3 source/scripts/compose_videos.py               # header + search videos
(cd source/preview && node render.mjs --device iphone && node render.mjs --device ipad)   # app previews
python3 source/scripts/check.py && python3 source/scripts/check.py --selftest
python3 source/scripts/contact_sheet.py
```

`render.py` takes about a minute per family; run families in parallel with `--only`
(each writes its own `build/textfit-<only>.json`). If a parallel run doesn't exit after
its last "rendered … zh-Hans" line, its output is already written — kill it.

## App changes (fixture only, not in this checkout)

The screens need believable content and no failed-upload rows, and the preview needs a
scripted walk through the app (the simulator can't be tapped from a script). That is a
DEBUG-only addition to the fixture driver, kept OUT of the main checkout:

- `App/Fixtures/StoreCreative.swift` (new): a synthetic 12 s screen recording of a
  workspace-setup web app (typing, two clicks, a toast), an editor project on it (two
  zooms, four caption lines, export 720p30), `-uiStoreEditor zoom|background|captions`
  and `-uiStoreTour` (library → project → editor → zoom selected → playback → export →
  share options).
- `App/Fixtures/FixtureDirector.swift`: launches those two flags.
- `App/Fixtures/FixtureWorld.swift`: `-uiFixtureNoInbox` hides the fixture inbox
  (an uploading and a FAILED take).

They live in a scratch worktree (detached from `main` at d2ec87c); the same change is
saved as `source/app-fixture.patch` (`git apply` it on a branch to reproduce).

## Design

Light, airy, Apple-style: the marketing site's `#fbfbfd` canvas with its violet /
blue / peach tints, SF Pro Display semibold, ink line + violet→blue gradient line.
One idea per screenshot, real app screens in a drawn bezel, no ratings, awards,
counts, prices, URLs or other products. The header uses the site's dusk-sky hero
gradient with white type ("Record your screen. Skip the editing."). Header and search
keep all words inside Apple's art safe areas (header 1646×661 at 1097,493; search
2168×1030 at 836,765 — from App Store Connect's Creative Assets Specifications); the
devices sit in the auxiliary area / straddle it. The app preview is pure app footage
(no title card, no overlays), so one file serves every locale.

## App previews (2026-11-20 rebuild, Strobia-style)

`source/preview/` is the Strobia app-preview pipeline (copied from the Strobia repo, not
linked) adapted to CaptureCat. It keeps what got Strobia's preview through App Review
after its v1 was rejected under guideline 2.3.4 (a phone drawn round the footage, a logo
at the end):

- the real app footage fills the frame: no device frame, no logo, no end card; the last
  frame is the app (the share link and its comments);
- a virtual camera makes one eased move per idea (library, the zoom block, the stage
  zooming as it plays, the Background pane and new gradient, the Subtitles pane, export
  and the share sheet, the share link page); every cut hides inside a punch-in, with
  motion blur (6 subframes per 60 fps master frame);
- one headline per idea from `copy.json` (ink + violet→blue), localized: each locale has
  its own file;
- the Strobia synth soundtrack (`soundtrack.py`), scored to the take's moments: a bell
  in, ticks and taps on the actions, swishes on the cuts, a chord resolving on the share
  page; stereo AAC 256 kbps;
- 30 fps from the 60 fps master by blending frame pairs; H.264 High 4.0, ~10 Mbps.

The take is `-uiStoreTour` (StoreCreative.swift), recorded by `scripts/record-tour.sh`
with `--mask=black` (unmask.py paints the corners and the Dynamic Island from their
neighbours). The simulator's recorder loses time while the export runs, so the shots
after it start where `footage/<device>-tour.cuts.json` says (read off the footage).

## Keyword headlines (2026-11-20)

Apple OCRs screenshot text for search, so every headline now carries the locale's
screen-recorder search term (`copy.json` → `_keywords`; the full table is
`source/build/keywords.txt`), kept whole on one line. `check.py` reads every screenshot,
header and search image with Apple's Vision OCR (`scripts/ocr.swift`, cropped round the
headline) and fails unless the term's stems are there; `--selftest` paints a headline out
and checks a German term against an English image, and both must fail. That check caught
the zh-Hans search line "录屏与" being read as "𣲛屏与", so the line became "录屏软件，".

## Localized app UI (2026-11-20, app at main 0a758c0)

The app is localized into all 12 languages, so every screenshot and preview now shows
the app in the locale's own language: `capture-shots.sh` / `record-tour.sh` take
`LOCALE=<store locale>` and launch with `-AppleLanguages (xx) -AppleLocale xx_YY`
(`scripts/locale-args.sh`) plus `-uiStoreLocalizedContent`, which localizes the fixture's
own content too (project and share names, comments, the recorded Acme web app, the demo
captions; `source/fixture-l10n.json` → `scripts/gen-fixture-l10n.py` →
`App/Fixtures/StoreFixtureL10n.swift` in store-wt). Each locale starts from cleared app
caches, because the fixture bakes project names into the thumbnails and movies it caches.
Shots live in `source/shots/<locale>/<family>/`, takes in `source/footage/<locale>/`
(previous English takes: `footage/v1`, `footage/v2-en`).

Previews render one locale per run from that locale's take
(`preview/render-previews.sh <iphone|ipad> [locales]`). The shots after the export start
where render.mjs finds them in the footage (scene changes), since the recorder drifts
from the step log during the export; `footage/<locale>/<device>-tour.cuts.json` can
override.

`check.py` also reads the app area of each non-English screenshot (below the headline)
and fails on English UI words (Export, Library, Capture, Background, …) unless the app's
own translation for that language uses the same word (e.g. German "Account"); the
selftest feeds it the English library shot as a German one.
